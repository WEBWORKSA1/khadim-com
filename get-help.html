What happens next

  1. We read your request

    Usually within a few hours. If something is unclear, we ask before passing it on.

  2. We check pay and the rules

    If your budget is below local rates or the arrangement needs a visa route, we tell you plainly.

  3. You get options

    Up to three licensed agencies or registered workers, or a step-by-step plan to hire directly.

  4. You decide

    No obligation. You interview, agree terms and pay the worker yourself.

Khadim does not charge workers any fee, and we only pass requests to agencies that agree not to charge workers recruitment fees either.

Before you send it

  • Check typical pay so your budget attracts good people.
  • Prepare interview questions.
  • Draft a contract in five minutes.
Do I pay Khadim anything?

No. Agencies you choose to use may charge you a placement fee, which they must tell you in advance.

What if I'm abroad and hiring for my parents?

Many requests come from families arranging elder care from overseas. Put your parents' city in the form and tell us who should be contacted locally.

Can I ask for a particular nationality or religion?

We don't filter by nationality, religion or caste. We match on skills, experience, languages, hours and the care your home needs.
