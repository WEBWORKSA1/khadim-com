How winners are chosen

A panel of three — a family employer, a domestic worker and a Khadim editor — scores each shortlisted story on care, skill, going beyond the job, and how long and how well the person has served. Nobody pays to enter or to win.

A quarterly challenge for workers. Film a two-minute video teaching one home skill — folding a fitted sheet, a five-ingredient dal, safely helping someone stand. The best three win US$150, US$75 and US$50, and we publish them on our video page.

Award rules

Who can be nominated?

Anyone aged 18 or over who works, or has worked in the past two years, as a domestic worker or home carer in a private household anywhere in the world. Khadim staff and their families are not eligible.

Is there a fee to enter?

No. Entry is free and no purchase is necessary. Prizes are funded by sponsors and reader support.

How are prizes paid?

Prizes are paid directly to the winning worker by bank transfer, mobile wallet or a method available in their country, in US dollars or local equivalent. Winners may need to confirm their identity. Where local law restricts prizes, an equivalent gift may be offered.

What happens to the stories?

Shortlisted stories may be published using the worker's first name, role and city only, and only with their written consent. Nominations are never shared for any other purpose.

When are winners announced?

Monthly winners are announced within the first ten days of the following month. The annual Home Hero is announced on 16 June, International Domestic Workers' Day. The judges' decision is final.

Can the awards change?

Khadim may change prize amounts or pause the awards with notice on this page. Any change does not affect nominations already received for a month that has closed.
