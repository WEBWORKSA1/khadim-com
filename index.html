Questions families ask

Is Khadim an agency?

No. Khadim is an information and matching service. We read your request, then connect you with licensed agencies or registered workers in your area, or give you a plan to hire directly. You agree terms and pay the worker yourself.

How much does it cost to use Khadim?

Nothing for households or workers. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships, agency listings and voluntary support from readers.

How do you work out the pay ranges?

We combine public agency rate cards, job listings, government minimum wages and household reports for each country, then round to typical ranges. They are estimates for budgeting, not legal minimums or quotes.

Will my phone number be shared?

Only with the agencies or workers you agree to, and only for your request. We never sell your details. See our Privacy Policy for how requests are handled.

Which countries do you cover?

We publish pay data and guides for India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Singapore, Hong Kong, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada, and accept requests from anywhere.

Can I hire someone directly instead of through an agency?

In India, the UK, the US and Canada, many families hire directly. In the Gulf, Singapore and Hong Kong, foreign domestic workers are usually recruited through licensed agencies or official platforms. Our country guides explain the legal route.
