Never pay to get a job. In the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and many other countries it is illegal for agencies or employers to charge workers recruitment fees. If anyone asks you for money to get a job, contact your country's labour ministry or embassy.

Know your rights before you say yes

  • A written contract in a language you understand, listing duties, pay, hours, rest days and leave.
  • Pay on time, in full, ideally into your own bank account. Keep a record of every payment.
  • Your documents stay with you. Employers should never keep your passport or ID.
  • Rest. A weekly day off and time to sleep. In the UAE that is at least 12 hours a day of rest; in Saudi Arabia 8 continuous hours.
  • Paid leave every year, and a ticket home every two years in most Gulf countries.
