A better deal on both sides of the front door

Around 75 million people worldwide work in other people's homes, according to the International Labour Organization, and most of them work informally. Families struggle to find reliable help; workers struggle to find fair employers. Khadim exists to fix both.

What the name means

"Khadim" is an everyday word in Arabic, Urdu, Hindi and Persian for a person who serves. We chose it because household work is service in the most literal sense — and service deserves respect, fair pay and clear terms. We use the word as a descriptive name only; see our trademark disclosure.

What we do

  • Free guides on hiring, pay, contracts, visas, safety and rights, written country by country.
  • Free tools — a fair pay planner, total-cost and gratuity calculators, and a contract builder.
  • A free matching service. Families tell us what they need; we reply within 24 hours with licensed agencies, registered workers or a plan to hire directly.
  • Free registration for workers, with a firm rule that no worker ever pays a fee through us.
  • The Fair Home pledge and Home Heroes Awards, which reward employers and workers who set the standard.

How we research

Legal facts come from official sources first — labour ministries, government portals, the ILO — and from reputable law firms and newspapers where official text is not available. Pay ranges combine public agency rate cards, job listings, government minimums and household reports, rounded to typical ranges. Every guide shows the date it was last checked and lists its main sources. Where the law is unsettled, we say so.

Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, tell us through the contact form. We check every report, correct errors promptly and note significant changes on the page.

How Khadim is funded

Advertising served by Google AdSense, clearly labelled sponsorships, agency listings and voluntary reader support. Advertisers and sponsors have no say over what we write. We never take money from recruiters that charge workers fees.

What we are not

Khadim is not a recruitment agency, an employer or a law firm. We don't employ the workers you hire, we don't hold money for either side, and our guides are general information, not legal advice. For decisions with legal consequences, check with the authority in your country or a qualified lawyer.
