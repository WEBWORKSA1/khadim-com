Keep fair-hiring help free for every family and worker

Khadim is independent. Your support pays for research and translations, the people who write and check our guides, the prizes for Home Heroes, and the outreach that brings these guides to workers who need them.

Where your money goes

UseShare of supportWhat it pays for
Operations25%Hosting, tools, security and keeping pay data current
Research & translation20%Checking laws each year; rights guides in Hindi, Arabic, Tagalog, Bahasa and Nepali
Awards & prizes20%Home Heroes and Skills on Camera prizes paid to workers
Promotion & outreach20%Reaching workers and families through community groups and ads
Hiring talent15%Writers, translators, video creators and fact-checkers

Target allocation. We publish a short report of money received and spent every quarter on this page.
