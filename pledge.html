The Fair Home pledge

Good workers choose good homes. These eight promises are based on the ILO Domestic Workers Convention (C189) and on what experienced helpers tell us matters most. Sign once, keep them always.

  1. A written agreement. Duties, pay, hours, rest, leave and notice, in a language they understand, signed by both of us.
  2. Fair pay, on time. At least the local legal minimum and the going rate for the role, paid in full each month on the agreed day, with a record.
  3. A weekly day off. At least 24 consecutive hours of rest every week, spent how and where they choose.
  4. Daily rest and sleep. Reasonable working hours, breaks, and uninterrupted rest at night.
  5. Paid leave. Annual leave, public holidays and paid sick days, with cover planned in advance.
  6. Their documents, their phone. Passports, IDs and phones stay with the worker. Always.
  7. A safe, private space. For live-in workers, a private lockable room, proper food and safe working conditions.
  8. Respect. No abuse or harassment, a fair hearing when things go wrong, and an honest reference when they leave.

Where the law where you live gives workers more than this, the law comes first.
