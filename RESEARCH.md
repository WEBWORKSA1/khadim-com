## 4. Verified facts used on the site

- UAE: Federal Decree-Law 9/2022 — weekly paid rest day, 12 hours' daily rest (8 consecutive), 30 days' paid leave, ticket home every 2 years; employer pays all recruitment and government fees; pay within 10 days; WPS mandatory for 5 roles incl. home caregivers; 6-month probation with refund rules; gratuity legally unsettled (14 days/year commonly applied).
- Saudi Arabia: Musaned for recruitment, contracts and transfers (3-party consent); electronic salaries mandatory from 1 January 2026; 8 hours' continuous rest, weekly rest day, a month's leave after 2 years, ticket every 2 years, up to 30 days' sick leave; one month's wage after 4 consecutive years; no charging workers, no passport confiscation.
- Qatar: Law 15/2017 — 10-hour cap, weekly rest, 3 weeks' leave, end of service at least 3 weeks per year after 12 months; QAR 1,000 minimum wage.
- Singapore: levy S$300 / S$450 / S$60 concessionary; S$5,000 bond; S$60,000 insurance; Settling-In Programme within 7 days; weekly rest with at least 1 non-compensable day a month; salary within 7 days of period end.
- Hong Kong: MAW HK$5,220 for contracts signed from 3 October 2026 (was HK$5,100); food allowance HK$1,236; 2-year Standard Employment Contract.
- India: Labour Codes in force from 21 November 2025; no comprehensive central domestic-work law; e-Shram had 2.79 crore domestic workers by March 2023; several states include domestic work in minimum wage schedules; police verification usually voluntary (e.g. Delhi Police online).
- ILO: 75.6 million domestic workers, 81% informal; C189 ratified by 40 countries (June 2026).

## 5. Trademark check

"Khadim's" / Khadim India Limited is a listed Indian footwear and accessories retailer. Khadim.com stays entirely in the household-services information field, uses the word descriptively, and states non-affiliation in the footer of every page and on `/trademark.html`.
