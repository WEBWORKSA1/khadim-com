Ways to work with us

📘

Sponsored guide

Your brand on a guide that matches your product — elder-care equipment on the elder care guide, payroll on the contract guide. Clearly labelled and editorially independent.

🏆

Award sponsor

Fund a month of Home Heroes or the annual award. Your name on the winner's story, certificate and newsletter.

✉

Newsletter placement

One sponsor slot in the monthly Home & Help letter, sent to families and agencies.

🤝

Service partner

Helper insurance, money transfer, background verification, training or payroll. Offer a deal to our readers.

🧭

Translation partner

Fund rights guides in a worker language and be credited on every translated page.

Website or domain

Interested in acquiring this website or the Khadim.com domain, or a joint venture? Contact the owner.

Our standards: we never accept advertising for recruitment that charges workers fees, for surveillance products aimed at workers, or for anything that conflicts with the Fair Home pledge. Sponsored content is always labelled. Display ads on the site are served by Google AdSense.

Who reads Khadim

  • Families in Indian metros hiring housekeepers, cooks, nannies and elder carers.
  • Indians and others living abroad arranging care for parents at home.
  • Households in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Singapore and Hong Kong employing live-in helpers.
  • Domestic workers and carers looking for fair jobs and their rights.
  • Licensed recruitment agencies and care providers.
