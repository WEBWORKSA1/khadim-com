Who can list

We list agencies that are licensed by the relevant authority — for example MOHRE or a Tadbeer franchise in the UAE, Musaned-registered recruitment offices in Saudi Arabia, MOM-licensed employment agencies in Singapore, the Labour Department in Hong Kong, or the state labour department where agencies must register in India.

Our fair recruitment rules

  • No recruitment fees or salary deductions charged to workers.
  • Workers keep their own passports and identity documents.
  • Written contracts in a language the worker understands.
  • Honest replacement and refund terms shown to families in advance.

Agencies that break these rules are removed. Families and workers can report concerns through our contact form.
