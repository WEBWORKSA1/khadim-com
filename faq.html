Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what families and workers ask us most. Each links to a full guide.

Using Khadim

Is Khadim free?

Yes, for households and for workers. We're funded by advertising, sponsorships, agency listings and reader support.

Is Khadim an agency or employer?

No. We're an information and matching service. You agree terms with and pay the worker or agency directly.

How quickly will I hear back after a request?

Within 24 hours, usually sooner. If your request is outside the areas we cover, we'll tell you and suggest the official route in your country.

Do you check workers' backgrounds?

We don't run background checks ourselves. We explain how to check identity, references and police records legally in your country, and we only refer families to licensed agencies.

Pay and costs

How much should I pay a full-time maid in India?

In metro cities a full-time housekeeper commonly earns around ₹12,000 to ₹20,000 a month in 2026, more for cooking or childcare. Smaller cities pay less. Use our pay planner for your city and duties.

What is the minimum wage for a foreign domestic helper in Hong Kong?

The Minimum Allowable Wage is HK$5,220 a month for contracts signed on or after 3 October 2026, plus free food or a food allowance of at least HK$1,236 a month.

What does a housemaid cost in the UAE beyond salary?

The employer pays recruitment and government fees, visa and medical costs, insurance, food and housing for live-in workers, and a ticket home every two years. Our total cost calculator adds it all up.

Should I give my helper a bonus?

In India an annual festival bonus of about one month's pay is customary. Elsewhere, a yearly raise and a bonus at a major holiday are good practice and help you keep good people.

Contracts and rights

Do I need a written contract for a part-time maid?

It's strongly recommended everywhere and required in many countries for live-in or migrant workers. A one-page agreement on duties, pay, hours, leave and notice prevents most disputes.

How many days off should a domestic worker get?

At least one full day a week. The ILO Domestic Workers Convention calls for 24 consecutive hours of weekly rest, and the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Singapore and Hong Kong all require a weekly rest day.

Can I keep my helper's passport for safekeeping?

No. Workers should always keep their own passport and identity documents. Holding them is banned in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and many other countries.

Is end-of-service gratuity due to domestic workers in the UAE?

The 2022 domestic worker law leaves this to Cabinet rules, and sources disagree. Many employers still pay 14 days' basic wage per year after one year. Check current MOHRE guidance before settling.

Safety and checks

How do I get police verification for a maid in India?

Most city police offer domestic help verification online or at the local station — for example Delhi Police through its citizen services portal. You submit the worker's photo, ID and address details. It is usually voluntary but widely recommended.

Can I install cameras to watch my nanny?

Rules vary. Cameras in shared areas are common, but tell the worker in writing, never place them in their private room or bathroom, and check local privacy law.
