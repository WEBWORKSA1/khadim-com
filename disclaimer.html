Laws change

Employment and immigration rules for domestic workers change often and differ by country, state and city. Always confirm with the official authority — for example a labour ministry, MOHRE, Musaned, the Ministry of Manpower, the Hong Kong Labour Department or your state labour department — or a qualified professional before acting.
