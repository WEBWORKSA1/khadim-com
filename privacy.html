Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Khadim.com ("Khadim", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses this website: households, workers, agencies, supporters and visitors.

Information you give us

  • Household requests: the help you need, your city, schedule, budget, household details you choose to share, your name, email and optional phone number.
  • Worker registrations: name, phone, location, skills, languages, experience, availability and optional details such as expected pay and work permit status.
  • Agency applications, nominations, pledges, donations, job applications, newsletter sign-ups and contact messages: the details in each form.

Please don't send identity numbers, passport copies, bank details or health records through our forms. We don't need them.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and handle your request.
  • With your consent, to share a household request with up to three licensed agencies or registered workers, or a worker's profile with matching families and agencies.
  • To send newsletters you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run the awards, process donation pledges and review applications.
  • To keep the website secure and understand, in aggregate, how it is used.

We do not sell personal information.

How forms are delivered

Forms are sent securely through a third-party form-processing service that delivers submissions to our private inbox. That service processes the data only to deliver it. Submissions are kept for as long as needed for your request — normally no longer than 24 months — and then deleted.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads are used. See our Cookie Policy for details of every cookie and how to control them.

Embedded videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you click play. Once played, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Storage on your device

Our tools save your choices (such as the pay planner, checklist progress and theme) in your browser's local storage so they're there when you return. This data never leaves your device unless you submit a form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law — you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. Use our contact form and choose "Privacy or data request". We reply within 30 days.

Children

This website is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Nominations and registrations for people under 18 are deleted.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We limit who can access submissions and delete them when no longer needed. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please share only what's needed.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be highlighted on the home page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form.
